La Salle Consulting Association (LSCA)

Co-founder · 2026 – present

Ferran Teixidor co-founded La Salle Consulting Association (LSCA) in 2026 to give students at La Salle Campus Barcelona a direct path into consulting: the skills firms test for, and the people who work there.

What LSCA does

  • Industry talks with professionals from consulting firms about what the job really looks like.
  • LSCA Consulting Academy, three hands-on sessions: problem solving and issue trees; case interviews; data, Excel and slide storytelling.
  • LSCA Case Competition (May 2027): teams solve a real brief from a company.
  • Consulting content on LinkedIn, written by the team.

My role

I set up the club's structure and its 2026-27 programme, lead member recruitment (applications and interviews), and run its content. Want in? Apply to LSCA.
